繁體中文

GR Pocket（以下稱「本 App」）是 RICOH GR III／GR IIIx 相機的非官方輔助工具。我們重視你的隱私：本 App 不收集、不上傳、不分享任何個人資料，也沒有帳號系統、廣告或分析工具。

照片

照片直接從你的相機透過相機的 Wi-Fi 傳到你的 iPhone，不經過任何伺服器。本 App 只要求「加入照片」權限，用來把你選擇匯入的照片存進「照片」App；不會讀取你既有的相簿。

位置

只有在你於設定中開啟「傳送位置至相機」後，本 App 才會使用 iPhone 的位置，並透過藍牙傳給你配對的相機，讓相機替之後拍攝的照片加上位置資訊。若你另外開啟「允許背景 GPS」，在 App 位於背景時也會持續傳送。位置只會傳給你的相機，本 App 不儲存、不上傳。你可以隨時在 App 或 iPhone 設定中關閉。

藍牙、Wi-Fi 與本機網路

本 App 使用藍牙連接你選擇的相機、同步時間與傳送位置；使用 Wi-Fi 與本機網路權限加入相機開啟的 Wi-Fi，並讀取相機中的照片。相機回報的 Wi-Fi 密碼只用於加入該網路，本 App 不會另外儲存。

存在 iPhone 上的資料

為了提供功能，本 App 會在你的 iPhone 上保存：已登記相機的名稱與識別碼、你的設定偏好，以及用來避免重複匯入的匯入紀錄（檔案內容雜湊值）。這些資料只存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 即會一併移除；匯入紀錄也可在設定中清除。

兒童

本 App 不針對兒童設計，也不收集任何人的個人資料。

政策變更

若本政策有更新，會在此頁面公布並更新日期。

English

GR Pocket ("the App") is an unofficial companion tool for RICOH GR III / GR IIIx cameras. The App does not collect, upload or share any personal data. It has no accounts, advertising or analytics.

Photos. Photos travel directly from your camera to your iPhone over the camera's own Wi-Fi, without any server. The App requests add-only Photos access to save the photos you choose to import; it never reads your existing library.

Location. Only after you turn on "Send location to camera" does the App use your iPhone's location, sending it over Bluetooth to your paired camera so the camera can geotag new photos. If you also turn on "Allow background GPS", this continues while the App is in the background. Location is sent only to your camera and is never stored or uploaded by the App. You can turn it off at any time in the App or in iOS Settings.

Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and local network. The App uses Bluetooth to connect to the camera you choose, sync its clock and send location, and uses Wi-Fi and local network access to join the camera's Wi-Fi and read its photos. The Wi-Fi password reported by the camera is used only to join that network and is not stored by the App.

Data kept on your iPhone. To work, the App stores on your device: names and identifiers of cameras you register, your preferences, and an import record (file content hashes) used to avoid duplicate imports. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the App; the import record can also be cleared in settings.

Children. The App is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes. Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
